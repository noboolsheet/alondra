# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents (often bilingual/trilingual, Latino and international families) in Miami-Dade and Broward County, South Florida, evaluating premium private music education for their children. They visit on phone or laptop, usually after a referral or search, in a considered "is this right for my child?" mindset — weighing quality, trust, and whether the investment is worth it. Their job to be done: decide that Alondra is the caring, credible choice, and book a trial class. Secondary audience: prospective instructors and referral partners forming an impression of the brand's professionalism.

## Product Purpose

Alondra offers premium private, at-home music lessons for children — world-class methods personalized per child, delivered by verified instructors across Miami-Dade and Broward. 12+ years, 500+ families served, trilingual (en/es/fr). The marketing site exists to convert considering parents into **booked trial classes** by establishing that Alondra is both nurturing (safe, caring, child-centered) and credible (serious expertise, verified teachers, proven outcomes). Success = a parent finishes the page reassured and books a trial. Trust is the on-ramp; the trial booking is the destination.

## Brand Personality

Warm & nurturing meets trusted & credible. Voice: caring but not saccharine, confident but not boastful — the tone of an accomplished teacher who genuinely loves children. Three words: **warm, credible, refined.** Emotional goal: a parent should feel *this is a place that will take my child seriously and treat them tenderly.* Reassurance over hype; specificity (verified instructors, real methods, real families) over adjectives.

## Anti-references

- **Childish daycare brands** — cartoon mascots, crayon scribbles, primary-color-block "kids' site" clichés. Alondra is for children but sold to discerning parents; the craft signals premium, the warmth signals care.
- **Cheap / discount positioning** — coupon energy, cluttered promos, anything that undercuts the world-class, high-touch price point.
- (Cross-register) Generic edu-tech/SaaS templates and corporate-conservatory stiffness are both off-brand: too cold for a brand whose product is warmth.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth is earned by craft, not cartoons.** Convey care through generous space, soft light, tender imagery of children and music — never through childish decoration.
2. **Show the proof, don't claim it.** Verified instructors, real families, concrete methods, real coverage. Specificity is what makes "premium" and "trusted" believable.
3. **Every path leads to the trial.** The book-a-trial action is the gravitational center; sections build the confidence that makes clicking it feel natural, not pushy.
4. **Trilingual parity is first-class.** en/es/fr are equal citizens; no language is a second-class translation. Copy and layout must hold at every language's word lengths.
5. **Premium restraint, not timid restraint.** Refined and calm, but committed — the deep-purple/warm-yellow identity carries real voice. Safe-and-invisible is a failure here.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (watch muted purple-gray on lavender), large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, meaningful alt text on all imagery. **Reduced motion is a first-class requirement** — every animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant). Trilingual delivery (en/es/fr) is core to inclusion for South Florida's multilingual families; `document.documentElement.lang` must track the active language.
